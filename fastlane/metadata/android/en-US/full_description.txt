Kestrel is an ADS-B plane-spotter that shows live air traffic around you.

Point your phone at the sky and the augmented-reality view labels the aircraft
overhead — on a synthetic sky or over the live camera feed — with callsign,
altitude, speed, and distance. A map view shows all nearby traffic with
size-class-coloured markers, and selecting a plane shows its altitude and
speed graphs plus route details.

Features:

* AR sky view (synthetic sky or live camera) driven by the phone's sensors
* Live map of nearby aircraft with recenter and layer controls
* Per-plane detail panel with altitude/speed history graphs
* Multiple map tile sources and three themes (Material You, Astro Red, Avionics Green)
* Imperial/metric units and configurable data radius

All data comes from free, keyless community APIs (adsb.lol) and open map tile
servers. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Location is used to center the sky/map on you. Camera is only used when you
switch to the live-camera AR view.
